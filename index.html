About Me

Coaching From the Inside Out

Movement has always been my language. From an athletic childhood to a career in performance, I learned early how deeply our bodies carry us through every season of life.

Now, as a midlife fitness coach, I help women adapt, rebuild, and grow stronger as they move through the natural changes of midlife. I believe strength is both physical and personal — and I'm passionate about empowering women to feel confident, capable, and at home in their bodies at any age.

Drawing from my 20+ years as an entertainer — fire dancing, roller skating, acrobatics — I understand how tough it can be to push your body while still seeking balance. That's why I coach you to ditch the all-or-nothing mindset and instead create a lifestyle that feels as good as it looks.

Whether you're starting fresh or leveling up, we'll build strength, boost energy, and bring out your healthiest, happiest self. It's time to put yourself first and embrace your power within!

The Method

What We're Building Together

Total Being Fitness

Total Being Fitness is about helping women find strength, inside and out.

I bring my background in entertainment, my personal experience navigating perimenopause, and a deep belief in sisterhood into the way I coach.

I've lived in my body through different seasons, and that shapes how I support my clients.

This work is about learning how your body responds, building trust with it, and having support while you do.

I don't believe women are meant to do this alone. When we train together, we become stronger together.

Total Being Method

The Total Being Method is a personal practice that honors the whole of who you are.

It blends intentional movement and strength training with meditation, mindfulness, and prayer, creating space for healing on physical, emotional, and spiritual levels.

This work is not about fixing yourself, but about remembering and reconnecting to what already exists within you. It invites you to slow down, listen deeply, and build strength that comes from alignment rather than force.

The practice is about clearing space in your life, letting go of habits that no longer serve you, and finding focus and direction toward the changes you're ready to make.

The Total Being Method is for those who feel called to go inward, to explore the quieter parts of themselves, and to honor the rhythms that make them uniquely who they are.
